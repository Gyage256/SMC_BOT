About SignalBot

A scanner that looks for trade setups on several exchanges and records them as paper trades: virtual entries with a stop loss and a 1:3 target. It never places real orders.

Strategies

SMC: break of structure or change of character, then price returns to the unmitigated order block.

DEMAND / SUPPLY: a small base candle followed by a strong impulse; a fresh zone that price revisits.

CRT: a daily candle sweeps the previous day's high or low and closes back inside; a 1h CHoCH confirms; entry at the order block.

PA: engulfing or pin bar at a recent swing level, in the direction of the EMA50 trend.

A+ means two or more strategies agreed on the same setup.

How to read the results

R is risk units: a stop-out is -1R, a target hit is +3R. Judge a strategy only after 100+ closed trades. Fees and slippage are not counted.
